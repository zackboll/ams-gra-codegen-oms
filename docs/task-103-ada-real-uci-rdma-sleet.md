## Positive and negative observations

The positive corpus has six documents: unchanged golden; IPv4-only
`198.51.100.42`; IPv6-only `2001:db8::42`; reversed IPv6/IPv4; four alternating
additional endpoints with additional security Choices; and empty optional
endpoints. Both XSDs authorize the last case. Encoding canonically omits the
empty optional sequence; independently publishing `Endpoint: []` is also
accepted and delivered by Sleet.

Each cell observes broker acceptance, actual typed delivery and independent
value/model checks. It has twelve primary deliveries (one externally published
recovery, five independently authored external variants and six generated
publications), one witness delivery and one decoder
rejection. After a second live typed subscription is accepted, the primary is
removed: publishing another golden reaches only the witness. Successful routing
to that witness, not just silence on a closed connection, proves unsubscribe.

The broker-accepted invalid timestamp is actually delivered to Ada and raises
`Decode_Error` without calling the typed handler. A valid unchanged golden then
reaches the **same live subscription**. This is real-Sleet recovery, not mock
evidence. Independently, all eight invalid payloads plus the timestamp are fed
directly into the generated decoder under each policy. Exact diagnostics are
asserted. Repeated-item failures deliberately expose the existing contextual
`Endpoint item 1`/`Endpoint item 2` diagnostics; they do not preserve every inner
constructor reason. Separate direct model probes require `Constraint_Error`
with exact UUID/version/IPv4/IPv6 constructor messages.

An earlier exploratory recovery candidate `:::` was accepted by **both** Sleet
and generated Ada: both honor the authentic IPv6 pattern's permissive lexical
space. This is not evidence of Ada rejecting malformed IPv6, and no stricter IP
parser was substituted. Exploratory failed assumptions/logs remain separate.
The successful recovery uses invalid dateTime instead. Broker `-ERR` cases are
never counted as Ada decoder execution.
